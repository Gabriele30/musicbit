## Demo audio

The diagnostics demo catalog references SoundHelix audio examples by T. Schürger, from [soundhelix.com/audio-examples](https://www.soundhelix.com/audio-examples). Source and attribution are included in the demo provider. The recordings are streamed from their original source and are not bundled in this repository.

Automated browser tests generate original PCM/WAV fixtures locally; they do not bundle catalog recordings.

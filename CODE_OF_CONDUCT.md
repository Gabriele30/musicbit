# Community guidelines

MusicBit welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Explain your reasoning, acknowledge corrections and help newcomers find the information they need.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, publishing another person's private information and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions or restrict participation when needed to keep the project welcoming. Reports can be sent privately using the maintainer's contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports will be treated with care and shared only as needed to address the situation.

These guidelines apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces maintained by the project.

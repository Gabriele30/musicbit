# Personal Home

Quick picks are ranked from the account's recent listening, favorites and completed/skipped signals. Blocked artists, dismissed tracks and tracks excluded from taste are omitted. The anonymous upstream Quick picks shelf is replaced rather than presented as personalized listening history.

Authenticated artist discovery uses the five highest-scoring artists in the editable Taste Profile. The configured playlist AI provider proposes new artist names; each name must match an artist search in the catalog before it becomes a navigable suggestion. Known and blocked artists are excluded. Artist portraits come from artist metadata, including hydration of references inferred from Home tracks.

Local Ollama discovery asks only for five artist names, with a 1024-token context and a 128-token output budget. Generating complete song titles and descriptions for artist discovery exceeded the timeout on the deployment's low-power CPU; the artist-only contract avoids that unnecessary inference work.

`GET /api/me/discovery/artists` returns `artists`, `basedOn`, `source` (`ai`, `related`, or `empty`) and `pending`. Related artists are returned immediately while one background AI inference has up to five minutes to finish. The active Home polls every fifteen seconds until the verified result is ready. On the deployment CPU, a direct three-name model probe took 45 seconds, so generation must not block the initial feed response.

Suggestions are cached separately by account, taste seeds and explicit artist preferences. Verified AI results last one hour; related results last ten minutes; empty results retry after one minute. Only one discovery inference runs at a time. Other requests can use related artists. AI failure retains catalog-related artists with a distinct UI label. An empty taste profile never sends an AI request. Server shutdown cancels pending model requests.

The Classifica section displays the daily Italy and Global Spotify rankings published by [Kworb](https://kworb.net/spotify/country/it_daily.html), with explicit source attribution, chart date, daily stream counts and links to the official [Spotify Charts](https://charts.spotify.com/home). `GET /api/charts/spotify?market=it|global` reads only these two fixed public upstream pages, validates consecutive ranks, track IDs and dates, and caches twenty entries per market for ten minutes. Data older than three days is labelled as an older observation. Upstream failures show a retry action and retain the official chart links; generic recommendations never replace chart positions. Selecting an entry searches MusicBit's catalog for the song; Spotify chart metadata does not grant or supply a Spotify audio stream. Integration tests disable live chart fetching with `MUSICBIT_CHARTS_ENABLED=false` and use explicit fixtures.

Wrapped uses bounded grid columns and truncates long ranking labels. On mobile, twelve months appear in two rows of six, without horizontal page or chart overflow. Browser checks cover widths 320, 375, 390 and 430 px with a full year of data and long track/artist names.
